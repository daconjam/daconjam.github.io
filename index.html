Dr. Jamell Dacon Computer Scientist, Health AI Researcher & Technical Program Leader

Dr. Dacon is a Ph.D. computer scientist advancing NLP, large language models, and trustworthy machine learning for high-stakes health challenges, including clinical decision support and health disparities. He pairs this research with a record of leading cross-functional technical teams and complex portfolios.
